ABOUT THE PROJECT

Collect reality.
Discover new connections.

A daily journal of possibilities hidden in everyday observations.

From fragments to three directions

Every evening at 21:00 Beijing time, a local agent reads unfinished items in the Apple Reminders list “世界碎片”. It looks for shared mechanisms, contradictions, unexpected parallels, and ideas that can travel between domains.

Each edition offers three directions worth exploring, with a new connection, an opportunity, an uncertainty, and a small experiment. The aim is discovery, not repetition.

Quiet when there is nothing to explore

No unfinished reminders means no edition and no notification. Completed items are excluded at query time. Original reminders are never edited, deleted, or published.

Distilled insights are delivered in the original chat and archived here. Browse by date to revisit earlier editions. Daily insights are currently written in Chinese.
